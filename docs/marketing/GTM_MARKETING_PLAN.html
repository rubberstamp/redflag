RedFlag Go‑to‑Market Plan

Version 1.0 – April 16 2025

1. Executive Summary

RedFlag is an AI‑powered platform that continuously monitors QuickBooks data to detect waste, fraud, and abuse. Our GTM strategy targets small‑to‑mid‑market businesses (10–250 FTEs) that rely on QuickBooks Online and have limited internal audit resources. We will leverage a self‑serve free assessment funnel, channel partnerships with accountants, and a focused outbound motion to establish RedFlag as the always‑on financial watchdog for growing companies.

3. Target Personas

Primary

  • The Vigilant CFO – Finance leaders responsible for risk mitigation and audit readiness.
  • The Time‑Strapped Controller – Accounting managers juggling month‑end close deadlines.

Secondary

  • Fractional / virtual CFOs servicing multiple SMB clients.
  • Accounting firms seeking value‑added advisory revenue.

4. Positioning & Messaging

Positioning Statement
For finance teams that need to sleep at night, RedFlag is the always‑on financial watchdog that catches fraud before it drains cash—unlike traditional audits that look months backward—because RedFlag continuously scans every transaction in real time.

Core Messages

  • Prevent losses, don’t just detect them after the fact.
  • Set‑up in five minutes via secure QuickBooks OAuth.
  • AI trained on billions of dollars in transactions—far beyond human pattern recognition.
  • Flat monthly pricing; cheaper than one hour of forensic accounting.

6. Channel Strategy

6.1 Self‑Serve Funnel

  • SEO & SEM for keywords like “QuickBooks fraud detection”.
  • Content magnets: Fraud ROI calculator, free playbook.
  • 14‑day in‑product trial of paid features.

6.2 Partner Program

  • Accounting & fractional CFO firms—25% rev‑share.
  • Co‑branded security one‑pagers to reassure clients.
  • Quarterly enablement webinars + MDF fund.

6.3 Outbound Motion

  • Small SDR team targeting 200 high‑risk verticals (Construction, Non‑profits).
  • Intent data (Bombora) + QBO App Store install lists.

11. Key Risks & Mitigations

  • Security distrust: Emphasize SOC 2 progress, third‑party pen‑tests, and read‑only OAuth.
  • False positives: Feedback loop for training models, human‑in‑the‑loop escalation.
  • Partner channel conflict: Deal registration + territory carve‑outs.
