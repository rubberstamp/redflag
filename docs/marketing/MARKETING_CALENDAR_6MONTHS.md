# RedFlag Marketing Calendar & Campaign Schedule
## First 6 Months After Launch

This document outlines the marketing activities, campaigns, and content calendar for RedFlag's first six months after launch. The calendar is designed to support the goals outlined in the GTM Strategy & Action Plan.

## Month 1: Launch & Initial Awareness

### Week 1: Launch Week
- **Launch Press Release**: "RedFlag Launches AI-Powered Financial Fraud Detection for SMBs"
- **Website Launch**: Complete website with assessment funnel goes live
- **Social Announcement**: Coordinated announcement across LinkedIn, Twitter, and industry forums
- **Email Campaign**: Announcement to seed list of potential users
- **Paid Campaigns**: Launch Google Ads and LinkedIn campaigns with initial targeting

### Week 2: Education Focus
- **Blog Post**: "5 Most Common QuickBooks Fraud Patterns" (SEO focus)
- **Email**: First nurture email to launch contacts
- **Content**: Publish "SMB Guide to Fraud Detection" (gated lead magnet)
- **Partner Outreach**: Initial outreach to top 20 accounting partners

### Week 3: Social Proof Building
- **Case Study**: First early adopter case study released
- **Testimonials**: Add first user testimonials to website
- **Social**: Share detection success stories (anonymized)
- **Webinar Promotion**: Start promotion for first webinar

### Week 4: First Webinar
- **Webinar**: "Introduction to RedFlag: How AI Detects Financial Fraud"
- **Email**: Webinar follow-up sequence
- **Blog Post**: "How to Interpret Your RedFlag Report"
- **Ad Optimization**: First round of ad creative optimization

## Month 2: Optimization & Partner Focus

### Week 1: Data Analysis
- **Campaign Analysis**: Review first month performance data
- **Conversion Optimization**: A/B testing landing page elements
- **Messaging Refinement**: Update messaging based on initial feedback
- **Partner Program**: Finalize partner program structure and materials

### Week 2: Partner Launch
- **Partner Portal**: Launch partner portal and resources
- **Partner Webinar**: "How to Offer Fraud Detection Services to Your Clients"
- **Email Campaign**: Partner recruitment campaign
- **Content**: "Partner Success Guide" (gated partner resource)

### Week 3: Vertical Focus 
- **Blog Series**: Launch "Industry Spotlight" series with Construction focus
- **Email**: Industry-specific campaign to construction companies
- **Ads**: Launch industry-specific ad sets
- **Case Study**: Construction company case study

### Week 4: Thought Leadership
- **Webinar**: "The State of Financial Fraud in 2025" with industry expert
- **Report**: Publish related data report (gated)
- **PR**: Pitch industry publications with key findings
- **Social**: Share key statistics as infographics

## Month 3: Scaling Successful Channels

### Week 1: Channel Expansion
- **Channel Analysis**: Identify top-performing channels from first 2 months
- **Budget Reallocation**: Shift budget to highest-performing channels

## Budget Allocation & Tracking

| Channel | Monthly Budget | KPIs | Tracking Method |
|---------|---------------|------|----------------|
| Google Ads | $5,000 | CPC < $5, CTR > 1.5%, Conv. Rate > 20% | Google Analytics + Ad Platform |
| LinkedIn Ads | $4,000 | CPC < $8, CTR > 1%, Conv. Rate > 15% | LinkedIn Campaign Manager + GA |
| Content Creation | $3,000 | Downloads > 500/mo, Conv. Rate > 25% | HubSpot + Google Analytics |
| Webinars | $1,500 | Registrations > 200, Attendance > 50% | Webinar Platform + CRM |
| Partner Marketing | $1,500 | Partner Sign-ups > 10/mo, Referrals > 20/mo | Partner Portal + CRM |

Total Monthly Budget: $15,000 ($90,000 for 6 months)
